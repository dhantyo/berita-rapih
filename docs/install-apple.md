# Berita Rapih on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Read Indonesian news without ads or pop-ups, with articles that are split over several pages merged into one. Free, no App Store, and **always the latest version**: both options below download the newest Berita Rapih each time you use them, so there's nothing to update.

The easiest way to install is the [install page](https://dhantyo.github.io/berita-rapih/#pasang), which shows the right steps for your device. Menu names below are as they appear on a device set to English, with the Indonesian name in brackets.

## Mac: one button in Safari

1. Open the [install page](https://dhantyo.github.io/berita-rapih/#pasang) in Safari and choose **Mac**.
2. If the Favourites bar isn't showing: **View → Show Favourites Bar** (⇧⌘B).
3. Drag the **Berita Rapih** button to the Favourites bar.
4. On a news article, click **Berita Rapih** in the Favourites bar.

The same button works in Chrome, Brave, Edge and Firefox on any computer (drag it to the bookmarks bar).

## iPhone and iPad

### Option 1: Shortcut (once it's published)

1. Open the Shortcut link on the [install page](https://dhantyo.github.io/berita-rapih/#pasang) and tap **Add Shortcut** (*Tambahkan Pintasan*).
2. **Settings → Apps → Shortcuts → Advanced** → turn on **Allow Running Scripts**
   (*Pengaturan → Apps → Pintasan → Lanjutan → Izinkan Menjalankan Skrip*).
3. On an article in Safari: **Share** (the square with an arrow) → **Berita Rapih**. The first time on each site, choose **Always Allow** (*Izinkan Selalu*).

Tip: in the Share sheet, scroll to the bottom → **Edit Actions** (*Edit Tindakan*) → add **Berita Rapih** to Favourites.

### Option 2: a bookmark (works today)

1. **Copy the link:** on the [install page](https://dhantyo.github.io/berita-rapih/#pasang), tap **Copy the Berita Rapih link**.
2. **Save that page as a bookmark:** open Safari's toolbar (tap **•••** at the bottom), then **Add Bookmark** (*Tambah Bookmark*) → **Save**. If it isn't there, tap **Share** first.
3. **Change that bookmark:** open your bookmarks (the book icon) → **Edit** → tap the bookmark you just saved. Change its title to **Berita Rapih**, delete the address that's there, and paste the link you copied. **Done**.
4. On a news article: open your bookmarks (the book icon) and tap **Berita Rapih**. Don't run it from the address bar: iPhone refuses it there with "Cannot Run Script". It also doesn't run in Private browsing.

## Use

The clean article appears over the page. The **gear** button sets text size, font, light, sepia or dark theme, and language. **Asli** takes you back to the original page. Supported: Detik, Kompas, Tribunnews (including its regional sites), CNN Indonesia, CNBC Indonesia, Liputan6, Merdeka, Suara, Okezone, Viva, iNews, JPNN, ANTARA, Republika, Bisnis.com, Tirto.

## If nothing happens

- Make sure the article is open in **Safari**, not in the built-in browser of WhatsApp, Instagram or X. In those apps, use the Safari or compass button, or **Open in Safari**.
- You need an internet connection: each run downloads the latest Berita Rapih (about 130 KB, cached by Safari).
- Homepages and section pages (not articles) aren't cleaned, on purpose.
- Settings (text size, theme) are saved per site, so set them once on each news site.
- Something wrong with an article? **Gear button → Beri masukan** (give feedback) sends it to us, with the link filled in.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your device. Berita Rapih has no server, no analytics, and doesn't know what you read. Each time you use it, it downloads its own code from GitHub Pages, and once a day the latest site rules; nothing about you or the page you're reading is sent.
